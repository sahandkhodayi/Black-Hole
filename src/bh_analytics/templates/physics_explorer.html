01 / PHYSICS

Why does the path bend?

In the Schwarzschild spacetime, a photon follows a null geodesic. With u = 1/r and the angle φ as the independent variable, the orbit equation becomes a second-order ordinary differential equation.

u′ = v   ·   v′ = 3u²/2 − u
02 / NUMERICS

How is it solved?

DOP853 is an adaptive, explicit Runge–Kutta method. It chooses internal step sizes to control local numerical error while integrating the coupled first-order system.

Initial state: u(0) = 1/r₀, v(0) = 0
03 / GEOMETRY

Two important radii

The event horizon is at r = 1 in our normalized units. The photon sphere is at r = 1.5, where circular photon orbits are possible. Scattering trajectories start outside it.
