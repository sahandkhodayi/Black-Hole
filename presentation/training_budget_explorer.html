Presentation note: The 1-minute and 10-minute PPO points are real test evaluations, but those runs used different reward functions and PPO settings. The later PPO points are a simple power-law scenario fitted to those two results. They must not be described as models actually trained for 1 hour, 10 hours, or 10 days.
MLP projection
The saved MLP run lasted 68.1 seconds and early-stopped at epoch 160 (best validation rollout at epoch 130). The graph holds its measured test score flat for longer budgets as a conservative plateau assumption; no longer MLP run was performed.
PPO projection
The dashed continuation uses a power law fitted to the 60.5 s and 600.1 s PPO test scores. Because the two runs had different configurations and only two anchor points exist, long-range uncertainty is high.
Metric
Normalized RMSE combines inverse-radius error u and derivative error v using the project's scales U_SCALE = 0.5 and V_SCALE = 0.2. Scores are averaged over five held-out test rays.
Scientific limitation
Five test rays are a small sample. These numbers compare the saved checkpoints on this task; they do not establish general superiority of neural networks over reinforcement learning.
